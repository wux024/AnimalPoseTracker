# Citation

Thank you for using AnimalPoseTracker. Here is what to cite, depending on which part of it
you use.

- **The toolbox as a whole** — [Wu et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2025.107685)
  (AnimalRTPose) and [Wu et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2025.112749)
  (AnimalViTPose), the two models it trains and exports.
- **The 3D stage** — [Wu et al. 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2022.104667) for the
  motion-parameter formulation, and
  [Wu et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/JSEN.2024.3374236) for the tracked-keypoint
  perception system it grew out of.
- **Pose without an image** — [Wu et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optlaseng.2025.109198)
  and [Wu et al. 2025](https://doi.org/10.3788/COL202523.091101) for single-pixel input, and
  [Liu et al. 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.optlaseng.2026.109715) for 3D human pose.
